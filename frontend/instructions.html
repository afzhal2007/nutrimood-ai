📘 Before You Start

How to use NutriMood AI

Follow these simple instructions to get better mood-based food recommendations and use Lucky AI safely.

01

Start Mood Check

Go to Mood Check and choose Face Detection, Voice Analysis, or Self Check. You can manually select your mood too.

02

Allow Camera / Mic

For face mood detection, allow camera access. For voice mood analysis, allow microphone access when the browser asks.

03

Get Food Suggestions

NutriMood AI suggests foods based on your mood, profile, goal, and food preference.

04

Ask Lucky AI

Ask Lucky AI for mood-based food tips, nutrition doubts, or general wellness suggestions.

05

Use Food Scanner

Upload a food image to preview basic nutrition details. Full AI scanning can be connected later with backend.

06

Track History

Your mood checks, food suggestions, and Lucky AI chats can be stored in History after login.

⚠️

Health Safety Note

NutriMood AI gives general food suggestions only. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions. For serious health problems, consult a doctor or dietitian.

🔒

Privacy Note

In this frontend version, your data is stored only in your browser using localStorage. Backend database storage can be added later.

🎯

Accuracy Note

Mood detection accuracy can change based on lighting, camera quality, background noise, and user input. Use self-check also for better results.
